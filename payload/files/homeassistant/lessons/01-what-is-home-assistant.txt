What is Home Assistant?

Home Assistant is free, open-source software that lives on a small computer in your house (a "home server") and
becomes the brain of your smart home. Lights, plugs, heaters, door sensors, cameras and speakers from many
different brands all show up in ONE place, and you can make them work together.

Why people love it
  - It works inside your house, so it still works when the internet is down.
  - Your data stays with you: no company sees when you come and go.
  - It talks to thousands of gadgets, so you are not locked in to one brand.

Where does it run?
  - A dedicated mini computer (Home Assistant OS on a Raspberry Pi or a small PC). This is the recommended way.
  - Or as a "container" on a server you already have (like your Immich server).
  - Or, to try it out, right here on this laptop (secure-linux-ha local start).

Important: Home Assistant is the server. This laptop has a window ("Home Assistant" in the app menu) that opens its
web page, and your phone has the Home Assistant app.
