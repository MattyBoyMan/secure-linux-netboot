Reaching Home Assistant from away (safely)

Your ranking, best first:
 1. NordVPN Meshnet (you already pay for it). Your phone and the server join a private network. Nothing is opened on
    your router. Cost: nothing extra. Needs: Meshnet working on the server and the phone.
 2. Cloudflare Tunnel plus Zero Trust Access. The server makes an outbound connection to Cloudflare; visitors must pass
    a login rule (your email plus a one-time code) BEFORE they even see Home Assistant. Cost: free for a few users.
    Needs: a domain on Cloudflare.
 3. Nabu Casa (Home Assistant Cloud). Paid, very simple, works with Alexa and Google, and it funds Home Assistant's
    developers. Trade-off: your connection passes through their servers (encrypted); it is a monthly cost.
NEVER: forwarding port 8123 or 443 on your router. Bots find these within hours.

Tell the checker which one you use: secure-linux-ha-secure --checklist
