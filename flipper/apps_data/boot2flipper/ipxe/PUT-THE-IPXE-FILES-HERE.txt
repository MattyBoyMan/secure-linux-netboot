PUT THE IPXE BOOT FILES HERE
============================

This folder must hold two files, downloaded from https://boot.ipxe.org :

  ipxe.efi    (for UEFI computers - almost every PC since about 2012)
  ipxe.lkrn   (for legacy BIOS computers)

So this folder then contains:
  apps_data/boot2flipper/ipxe/ipxe.efi
  apps_data/boot2flipper/ipxe/ipxe.lkrn

They are not included in this repository because they are programs built
by the iPXE project; getting them from boot.ipxe.org is the official way.
