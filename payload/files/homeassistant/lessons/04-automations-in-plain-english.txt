Automations in plain English

An automation has three parts:
  TRIGGER     what starts it ("the motion sensor sees movement", "the sun sets", "it is 7:00 am")
  CONDITION   optional gate ("only if it is dark", "only if someone is home")
  ACTION      what it does ("turn on the hallway light", "send me a notification")

Three ways to make one, easiest first:
  1. Blueprint.  Settings > Automations & scenes > Blueprints. Pick the recipe, choose your sensor and lights from the
                 lists, save. No typing. We ship several (motion lights, sunset lights, away mode, goodnight, vacation).
  2. The editor. Settings > Automations & scenes > Create. Pick the pieces from menus.
  3. YAML.       Text files. Most powerful, easy to break; use secure-linux-ha validate before restarting.

Tip: secure-linux-ha-learn explain <file> reads any of our files aloud in plain English, so you can learn by
looking at working examples.
Tip: change one thing at a time and test it before moving on.
