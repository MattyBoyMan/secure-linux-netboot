Welcome to Secure Linux (live session)
======================================

You started your computer from the Secure Linux drive. This is a "try it" session.

What is safe about it
- Nothing is installed. Your computer's own disks are not opened, changed or erased.
- When you switch off, everything you did here is gone, unless this drive has "keep my files" space.
- Nothing is sent anywhere on its own.

What you can do from here
  1. Read the guide          secure-linux-live-session guide
  2. Check this drive        secure-linux-live-session check-drive
  3. Install Secure Linux    secure-linux-live-session installer   (shows the steps; it does not start anything)
  4. Start NOMAD             secure-linux-live-session nomad       (offline library, if you have it)

Or type   secure-linux-live-session   on its own to get a small menu.

If you only want to try it, just look around and switch off when you are done.
