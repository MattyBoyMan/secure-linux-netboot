The key words you need

Entity       One thing Home Assistant can see or control: a light, a temperature reading, a door sensor.
             Names look like light.lounge or sensor.hall_temperature. (domain.name)
Device       The physical gadget. One device can have several entities (a motion sensor also reports battery).
Integration  The "driver" that lets Home Assistant talk to a brand or a standard (Zigbee, Hue, Matter, ESPHome).
Area         A room: Kitchen, Bedroom. Put devices in areas and you can say "turn off the kitchen".
Automation   "When this happens, do that." A trigger, optional conditions, and actions.
Script       A saved list of actions you run on demand. Like a macro.
Scene        A saved look: "Movie night" sets these lights to these levels.

Three more you will meet soon
Helper       A little switch, number or text box you create yourself. Great for modes ("Away mode").
Blueprint    A ready-made automation recipe where you only choose the sensor and lights from lists.
Dashboard    The pages you look at. Yours to arrange.
Add-on       Extra software that runs next to Home Assistant (only on Home Assistant OS).
