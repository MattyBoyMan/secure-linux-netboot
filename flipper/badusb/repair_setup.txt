REM Flipper Zero BadUSB: re-checks and repairs the setup on a computer installed with Secure Linux
REM (from the USB stick or over the internet - the installed system is the same).
REM Copy to the Flipper SD card under /badusb/ (qFlipper does this). Only use on your own computers.
DELAY 1000
CTRL-ALT t
DELAY 2000
STRING sudo /usr/local/lib/secure-linux/setup.sh --repair
ENTER
