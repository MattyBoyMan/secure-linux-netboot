Your first 30 minutes

1. Open it.          Home Assistant (app menu), or the address in your browser. First time: make the owner
                     account. Use a long unique password from your password manager. This is the admin.
2. Make a second     Settings > People > Add person. Give yourself a normal (non-admin) account for daily use.
   account.
3. Turn on 2-step    Profile (bottom-left) > Security > Multi-factor Authentication > Enable. Use an
   login.            authenticator app. Do it for every account.
4. Set your home.    Settings > System > General: location, time zone (Australia/Melbourne), units, currency AUD.
5. Look at what      Settings > Devices & services. Home Assistant often discovers lights and speakers by itself.
   it found.         Click Configure on what you recognise.
6. Add your phone.   Install the Home Assistant app (Android) and sign in. It gives you notifications and presence.
7. Switch on         Settings > System > Backups > Set up automatic backups. Keep encryption on. Save the key in
   backups.          your password manager.
8. Run the           secure-linux-ha-secure --check . Fix anything marked NEEDS FIXING.
   security check.

Stop there. Everything else can wait for another day.
